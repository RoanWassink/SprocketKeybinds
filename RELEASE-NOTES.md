A shared keybind menu for Sprocket mods, grouped by author or declared mod pack.

## What changes for you

Configure mod controls inside the vanilla Settings menu. Expand author/mod groups, switch between author and pack grouping, and use Clear, Reset, Apply, Accept and Cancel. Personalized bindings survive updates.

**Beta:** tested together in the Cold War pack. Armour-response values are bounded gameplay approximations, not exact historical protection or a guarantee against every shell.

## Requirements and update

Sprocket 0.2.55.5, Windows x64 and an already-working Sprocket Mod Loader / BepInEx 6 IL2CPP setup. **Loader not included. Quality of Life not required.**

Close the game, back up matching files and saves, then merge the ZIP's folders into the game directory. Keep one DLL per plugin and preserve customized configs/catalogues/WAV overrides. See [README](https://github.com/RoanWassink/SprocketKeybinds#readme) for exact use, controls, limitations and uninstall instructions.
